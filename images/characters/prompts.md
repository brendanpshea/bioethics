# Chibi Character Sheets: Image Prompts

Four 3×3 sheets, 36 figures in all, for dialogue slides: a patient and a clinician (or two clinicians) facing each other, with speech balloons added on the slide rather than in the image.

Each prompt below is self-contained, so you can paste it into any image generator as is. Ask for a **square image** (1:1, at least 1536×1536) so the nine cells come out equal.

## Design rules (already built into every prompt)

- **Background:** flat, solid magenta `#FF00FF`, with no gradient, floor, shadow, or grid lines. Magenta keys out cleanly.
- **No magenta in the figures:** no pink, purple, or violet clothing, hair, or props. Blush stays a faint peach. Otherwise those pixels vanish when the background is removed.
- **One direction:** every figure faces three-quarters toward the viewer's right. To make two characters face each other, flip one horizontally. That is also why the figures carry no text, logos, or name badges, which would read backwards after flipping.
- **One scale:** all heads are the same size, and children are visibly shorter. In each row, feet sit on the same invisible baseline, so any two figures can stand side by side.
- **Neutral expression:** pleasant, attentive, mouth closed, so one figure works for many conversations. Expression variants can come later (see the end of this document).
- **Dignity:** the variety in age, skin tone, gender, body type, faith, and disability should look ordinary, never caricatured. Disabled characters are people doing things, not props.

## After generating

1. **Check each sheet** before splitting: count nine figures, and look for stray text, pink or purple clothing, figures touching the cell edges, and mismatched head sizes. Re-roll the whole sheet, or regenerate a single figure with the same style paragraph and just that one cell's description.
2. **Split and key:** cut the sheet into a 3×3 grid of equal cells, turn magenta pixels transparent, then trim each figure to its bounding box. Expect a thin magenta fringe on the outlines that needs removing or eroding; the thick dark outlines make that easy.
3. **Name files by role and number,** for example `nurse-01.png` … `patient-09.png`, `family-04.png`, and keep a short `cast.md` listing who each figure is. That way a slide can say "Daniel" and point to `patient-01.png`.
4. **Flip for conversation:** mirror the right-hand speaker horizontally so the two face each other. Add the balloons as slide markup, not in the image, so the dialogue stays editable and searchable.
5. **Licensing:** before these go into a CC BY-NC textbook, check that your image generator's terms let you publish its output under that license.

## Possible later sheets

- **Expressions:** six to eight recurring characters, each showing three expressions (listening, worried, explaining).
- **Settings:** a few simple flat backgrounds (exam room, hospital corridor, pharmacy counter) in the same style, for placing pairs into a scene.
